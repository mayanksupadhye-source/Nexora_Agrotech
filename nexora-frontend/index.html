The Smart Solution

A Direct B2B Marketplace
Built for Bharat

One platform. Zero middlemen. Full transparency. Nexora removes the middle layer and connects farmers directly to buyers.

01
📲

Farmer Listings

Farmers upload crop details — type, quantity, location — and set their expected price or accept competitive bids from verified buyers.

02
🔍

Industry Search & Bidding

Factories and bulk buyers search by crop, region, and quantity. They contact farmers directly and place bulk orders — no phone tree, no broker.

03
🤖

Smart Matching Engine

Our AI suggests the best farmer-buyer pairs based on location proximity, demand patterns, and live price trends in the market.

04
📊

Live Price Transparency

Every farmer can see what industries are actually paying today. No more guessing. No more manipulation. Just fair market data.

05
🛡️

Verified B2B Trust Layer

Every buyer is verified. Every transaction is tracked. Farmers deal only with legitimate factories and certified buyers.

06
📱

Mobile-First, Low-Tech Friendly

Built for rural India. Works on low-end smartphones, supports regional languages, with a UI any farmer can use in minutes.

07
🌱

AI Crop Advisory

Get instant, AI-driven guidance on soil health and crop suitability. Nexora verifies soil conditions and recommends what to grow, when to sow, and how to improve yield.

08
📸

Quality Transparency

Farmers submit photos of their harvest and our AI analyzes crop quality automatically. Buyers can view the real photos themselves before placing a bid — no surprises, no disputes.

09
💬

Live Chat, Powered by Socket.io

Real-time messaging between farmers and buyers lets both sides negotiate, confirm details, and close deals instantly — direct conversation, zero delay.
